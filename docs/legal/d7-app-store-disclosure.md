# D7 App Store / Google Play auto-renewal disclosure

This is the Apple D7 / Google Play auto-renewal disclosure text.

It is kept in the repository for App Store and Google Play submissions. Do not render it on the web preview, and do not reuse it for the Stripe or web billing version. The Stripe launch needs a separate web version under New York GBL §527-a, which Legal will write when billing goes live.

The same string is exported as `D7` from `js/notices.js` for the store integration. It is not written into the page.

```
Draw Buddy Premium: $1.99 per month or $17 per year. Your subscription renews automatically at the same price each month or year until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account. Cancel anytime, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in Settings > your name > Subscriptions or Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or from Draw Buddy's Account screen.
```
