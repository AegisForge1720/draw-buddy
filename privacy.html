Privacy

Draw Buddy is provided by NAV Analytics LLC. Last updated November 16, 2026.

No accounts

Draw Buddy has no sign-up or login. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

What stays on your device

Draw Buddy saves the following in your browser's storage on this device only:

  • Your 18+ confirmation, remembered for 30 days. Tapping "I'm under 18" clears it.
  • Tickets and slips you log, and any ticket photo you add to a slip (the photo is shrunk and kept with the slip).
  • Your chosen venue, recent and favorite venues, and venue check-ins.
  • Your settings, such as the selected game, state, bankroll, open sections and your Extra wheel direction log.

You can remove all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Location ("Near me")

Draw Buddy never asks for your location when it loads. It asks only when you tap 📍 Near me or check in to a venue by location.

  • By default, your position is compared on your device with a list of NY Quick Draw bars and restaurants included in the app, and it is not saved.
  • Location check-in is also checked on your device.
  • If you turn on "Show all Quick Draw retailers" and tap Near me, your coordinates are sent to data.ny.gov (New York State's open data site) to find retailers within about 5 miles.

Services the app loads from

When you use Draw Buddy, your browser makes requests to these services. Like any website, they receive standard request details such as your IP address and browser type.

  • GitHub Pages: hosts this site and keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses.
  • Supabase: to read official draw history.
  • data.ny.gov: New York State open data, the source of the draw results and the NY Lottery retailer list.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): loads the Supabase code library.
  • Tailwind (cdn.tailwindcss.com): loads the page styles.

Draw Buddy has no ads, no tracking pixels and no analytics, and we don't sell or share your information.
